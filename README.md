### Paracosm - An offline note taking site made by a system, for systems. 

hey! my name is ezra, and i am the developer of Paracosm. Paracosm is a website made for systems to log alters, take notes, and stay organized. unlike most system focused apps, Paracosm runs entirely in your browser and stores data locally on your device. no account is required, and nobody, not even me, can access your data other than you.

key included features:

- track alters and system members
- log fronts and co-fronts
- keep thoughts organized with custom boards
- leave messages for your alters
- archive old boards without losing data
- customize terminology to fit your system
- easily export your data for backups or moving between devices
- import from simply plural, pluralkit, or pluralspace
- sync to and from pluralkit with one button
- lock boards behind a password, and only allow certain alters to see selected boards

frequently asked questions

Q: is this site open to systems of all origins?
A: yes! i aim to be inclusive to *all* systems. i am very pro endo, and my site is as well.

Q: is donation necessary/required? 
A: of course not. i will never paywall any core features of my site, and i will never make my site pay to use. currently, all donations do is support me (currently unemployed), and give you a special role in the server.

Q: will there ever be features like friends or viewing others systems? 
A: no. im planning on keeping this site entirely browser based and private, with no online features like those. i personally believe its for the better this way, as sharing system details so readily can be unsafe, and i prioritize the safety and privact of my users over features like these. sorry! 

Q: will Paracosm ever be an app? 
A: probably in the far future. im an unemployed teenager, and i cannot afford the 99$ a year fee to publish an app on the app store, and whatever android needs. also, i am not well educated on the coding languages used to make non “web based” apps. possibly, when i have saved up money and learned how to create apps like this, i would love to publish an app :) but for now, Paracosm is soley web based.
