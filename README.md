### Paracosm — an offline note-taking site made by a system, for systems

hey! my name is ezra, and i'm the developer of Paracosm. Paracosm is a website made for systems to log alters, take notes, and stay organized. unlike most system-focused apps, Paracosm runs entirely in your browser and stores data locally on your device. no account is required, and nobody — not even me — can access your data other than you.

#### try it out

Paracosm is hosted on GitHub Pages, so there's nothing to install or clone. just visit the link below and start using it:

🔗 **paracosm-app.github.io/landing**

#### key features

- track alters and system members
- log fronts and co-fronts
- keep thoughts organized with custom boards
- leave messages for your alters
- archive old boards without losing data
- customize terminology to fit your system
- easily export your data for backups or moving between devices
- import from Simply Plural, PluralKit, or PluralSpace
- sync to and from PluralKit with one button
- lock boards behind a password, and only allow certain alters to see selected boards

#### frequently asked questions

**Q: is this site open to systems of all origins?**
A: yes! i aim to be inclusive to *all* systems. i am very pro-endo, and my site is as well.

**Q: is donation necessary/required?**
A: of course not. i will never paywall any core features of my site, and i will never make my site pay-to-use. currently, all donations do is support me (currently unemployed), and give you a special role in the server.

**Q: will there ever be features like friends or viewing others' systems?**
A: no. i'm planning on keeping this site entirely browser-based and private, with no online features like those. i personally believe it's for the better this way, since sharing system details so readily can be unsafe — and i prioritize the safety and privacy of my users over features like these. sorry!

**Q: will Paracosm ever be an app?**
A: probably in the far future. i'm an unemployed teenager, and i can't afford the $99/year fee to publish on the App Store, or whatever Android needs. i'm also not well-educated on the languages used to build non-"web based" apps. possibly, when i've saved up money and learned how to build apps like this, i'd love to publish one :) but for now, Paracosm is solely web-based.

#### a note on privacy

since Paracosm stores everything locally in your browser, your data never touches a server — which also means it isn't backed up anywhere but your device. **clearing your browser data, switching browsers, or switching devices can erase your boards for good.** use the export feature regularly to keep backups you can restore from.
