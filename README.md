### Paracosm — an offline note-taking site made by a system, for systems

hey! my name is ezra, and i'm the developer of Paracosm. Paracosm is a website made for plural systems to log alters, track fronting, take notes, and stay organized — all without ever creating an account or trusting your data to someone else's server. everything runs entirely in your browser and stores data locally on your device. no signups, no logins, and nobody — not even me — can access your data other than you.

#### try it out

Paracosm is hosted on GitHub Pages, so there's nothing to install or clone. just visit the link below and start using it:

🔗 **paracosm-app.github.io/app**

first time using it? Paracosm has a short guided onboarding flow that walks you through importing existing data, picking your terminology, and setting up accessibility options — or you can skip it and dive straight in.

#### key features

**system & alter management**
- detailed alter profiles with names, pronouns, roles, proxy tags, colors, avatars, and custom fields
- organize members into **groups** and **subsystems** — subsystems can even track their own independent front
- build out a relationship map between members with a visual relationship graph
- archive old alters or boards without losing their data

**fronting**
- log who's fronting (including co-fronting) and keep a full history of switches
- customizable dashboard with widgets so you can see what matters most at a glance

**boards & communication**
- custom message boards for notes, journaling, and communication between alters
- pin important posts, attach images, and compose as any member of your system
- lock individual boards behind a password, and restrict which alters are allowed to view them

**personalization**
- fully customizable terminology — rename "alters," "fronting," "boards," and more to fit how your system talks about itself
- light and dark mode, adjustable font size and font family, and a range of other accessibility options
- adjustable dashboard layout, including column count on mobile

**data ownership**
- everything is stored locally — no account, no server, no tracking, no third parties involved
- export your data anytime as a full JSON backup for safekeeping or moving between devices
- import from Simply Plural, PluralKit, or PluralSpace to bring your existing records over in minutes
- one-button sync to and from PluralKit, so the two can stay up to date with each other
- periodic backup reminders nudge you to export before you forget

#### frequently asked questions

**Q: is this site open to systems of all origins?**
A: yes! i aim to be inclusive to *all* systems, of any origin — whether you're traumagenic, endogenic, or anything in between. i am very pro-endo, and my site is as well.

**Q: is donation necessary/required?**
A: of course not. i will never paywall any core features of my site, and i will never make my site pay-to-use. currently, all donations do is support me (currently unemployed), and give you a special role in the server.

**Q: will there ever be features like friends or viewing others' systems?**
A: no. i'm planning on keeping this site entirely browser-based and private, with no online or social features like those. i personally believe it's for the better this way, since sharing system details so readily can be unsafe — and i prioritize the safety and privacy of my users over features like these. sorry!

**Q: will Paracosm ever be an app?**
A: probably in the far future. i'm an unemployed teenager, and i can't afford the $99/year fee to publish on the App Store, or whatever Android needs. i'm also not well-educated on the languages used to build non-"web based" apps. possibly, when i've saved up money and learned how to build apps like this, i'd love to publish one :) but for now, Paracosm is solely web-based.

**Q: can I import from PluralKit?**
A: yep — just paste in your PluralKit token (using `pk;token` in a server with the PluralKit bot) and Paracosm will pull in your alters. you can also sync back to PluralKit at any time with a single button.

**Q: is Paracosm open source?**
A: yes, completely. the code is public on GitHub if you'd like to look through it, report bugs, or contribute.

#### a note on privacy

since Paracosm stores everything locally in your browser, your data never touches a server — which also means it isn't backed up anywhere but your device. **clearing your browser data, switching browsers, or switching devices can erase your boards for good.** use the export feature regularly to keep backups you can restore from — Paracosm will also periodically remind you to back up, just in case.
