A system manager

Paracosm is a private, local-first tool for tracking alters, fronting history, system boards, and more — all in your browser, no account needed.

what's in it

everything stays in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

🌿

Alter profiles

Names, pronouns, roles, proxy tags, colours, and custom fields.

✦

Fronting tracker

Log who's fronting and keep a history of switches.

📋

System boards

Message boards for notes and communication between members.

🔒

Local-first

No account, no server, no tracking. Just your browser.

🌙

Accessibility Features

Light & dark mode, text size, font, and many more accessibility options.

📦

Import & export

PluralKit, Simply Plural, and Pluralspace import along with a JSON backup built in.
